# CineMatch original artwork — v1.4.2

These 22 local SVG compositions were authored for CineMatch with geometric
paths, gradients and original arrangements. They are **original illustrations,
not official film posters**. No external images, fonts, logos, character
likenesses, TMDB assets or third-party poster files are included. They are
covered by the repository's MIT license. Film names identify catalog entries;
they do not imply endorsement.

`app/cinematic_artwork.py` explicitly maps existing Wikidata film identities to
these assets. The mapping is presentation only: `assets/demo/catalog.json`,
provider metadata, profile exports and schemas are unchanged. Each illustration
is embedded locally as SVG data, so no image service or network call is needed.
The localized film name and “Original artwork” notice are accessible HTML text.

Existing nonempty provider posters take precedence, including all eight TVmaze
series. Unknown titles retain the neutral fallback. Turning posters off also
turns these illustrations off. Cards and details use the same rendering path.

The compositions use independently drawn visual motifs: orbital light, desert
layers, ice and repetition, organic division, emotional color fields, a racing
line, city reflections, a nocturnal archway, a documentary window, a winding
road, neon, forest machinery, a musical night, water, a stellar ring, a doorway,
a hovering form, a leaf, pastel architecture, dunes, a digital threshold and
intersecting orbits. They do not reproduce official poster compositions.
